Our story

History with a human heartbeat.

Viking Legacy & Lore, hosted by T.R. Pomeroy, explores the Viking world through narrative history, archaeology, Norse mythology and the sagas.

We follow the people behind the legends: their voyages and ambitions, their beliefs and laws, and the stories they left behind.
