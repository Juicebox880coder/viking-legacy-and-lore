Contribute

Bring a story to the table.

Have a Viking-world story worth exploring? Send a short idea by email. We are gathering suggestions for the podcast and the future written publication.

Start with a short pitch

Tell us the central question, why the story interests you, and what a listener or reader would take away. A paragraph or two is enough to begin.

Give us a trail to follow

Include the sources you would use, with links or publication details. If the idea involves an image, translation or quotation, identify its creator and where it was published.

Tell us about your perspective

Add a short introduction and any relevant experience or previous work you would like to share. Ideas from curious readers are welcome alongside proposals from writers and researchers.

What happens next

Email is the current route for ideas; there is no contributor account or upload portal. Sending a pitch does not guarantee a response or publication. Scope, credit, permissions and any payment would need to be discussed before commissioned work begins.
