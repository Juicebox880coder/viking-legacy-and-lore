Norse Library

A reading room for the curious.

The Norse Library is being prepared as a guide to books, collections and sources. It is not yet a searchable catalogue or a collection of full texts.

Books & scholarship

The planned reading guides will give each work context: who wrote it, what it covers and where a reader might begin.

Museums & archaeology

A future collection of museum resources and archaeological discoveries will connect readers with the institutions and publications behind them.

Sagas & Eddas

Future guides will identify texts, editions and translators so readers can understand which version they are reading. Full-text readers and translations are planned for a later stage.

Help shape the shelves

Recommend a book, a museum collection or a source through the contact page. Include its title, author or institution, and a link where available.
