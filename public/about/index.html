About

History with a human heartbeat.

Viking Legacy & Lore follows the people behind the legends: their voyages and ambitions, their beliefs and laws, and the stories they left behind.

The voice behind the stories

Hosted by T.R. Pomeroy, the podcast and YouTube channel explore Viking history, archaeology, Norse mythology and the sagas. Each episode offers another way into the Norse world, from everyday ingenuity to the stories that still stir the imagination.

History, lore and imagination

Historical evidence, myth and saga offer different kinds of insight. Our aim is to make those distinctions clear while keeping the human story in view. Original Tora stories sit in their own episode category, alongside the history and mythology archive.

A publication taking shape

The listening room and Norse Library are open. Follow credited resources through guided learning paths, or explore archaeology case studies that distinguish evidence from interpretation. Articles are the next chapter of the written publication.

Keep the conversation going

Have a question, a source to recommend or a correction to an episode? Include the episode title and the passage or timestamp so we can follow your reference.
