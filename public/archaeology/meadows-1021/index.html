L’Anse aux Meadows: a date for an activity

A precise date can transform a discussion without answering every question about a settlement.

Newfoundland, Canada · AD 1021

Study online 2021; journal issue 2022 · Sources reviewed 2026-09-13

Evidence

Kuitems and colleagues used the radiocarbon signal associated with AD 993 and tree-ring measurements to date Norse activity at the Newfoundland site to AD 1021. [1]

Interpretation

The study supplies a firm chronological point within the history of Norse activity in the Americas. Read it alongside the archaeological site record. [1] [2]

What remains open

This date alone does not establish first arrival, the total length of occupation, or which named saga traveller was present. Those are separate historical questions. [1]
