Gjellestad: from radar trace to investigation

A case study in how one kind of investigation leads to another.

Halden, Norway · Early Viking Age, as reported in 2020

Radar discovery 2018; report updated 2020 · Sources reviewed 2026-09-13

Evidence

NIKU reports that ground-penetrating radar detected the ship in 2018. Its January 2020 update describes preliminary investigation and timber dating supporting an early Viking Age attribution. [1]

Interpretation

The agreement between survey results and later investigation supports the identification of an archaeological ship. The report connects its setting with burial mounds and buildings. [1]

What remains open

Radar shapes do not provide a complete biography of a site. This entry covers the 2018–2020 milestone and does not present it as the project’s latest excavation report. [1]
