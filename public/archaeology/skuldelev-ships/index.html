Skuldelev: ships made into a barrier

A vessel’s final use may be very different from the purpose for which it was built.

Roskilde Fjord, Denmark · 11th century

Ships excavated 1962 · Sources reviewed 2026-09-13

Evidence

The Viking Ship Museum describes five deliberately sunk ships forming a barrier in Roskilde Fjord. Its account dates the first phase to about 1060–1070 and records excavation in 1962. [1]

Interpretation

The museum suggests protection from seaborne attack or control of traffic as possible purposes. Its reconstructions draw on documented surviving parts. [1]

What remains open

Missing and deformed timbers make reconstruction an interpretive process. A reconstructed vessel should be distinguished from the archaeological remains on which it is based. [1]
