The Galloway Hoard: objects and their wrapping

The materials around an object can be evidence in their own right.

Dumfries and Galloway, Scotland · Buried around AD 900

Discovered 2014 · Sources reviewed 2026-09-13

Evidence

National Museums Scotland describes a hoard discovered in 2014, buried around AD 900, with precious objects and surviving textiles and leather around them. [1]

Interpretation

Studying those materials can help researchers investigate how objects were gathered and prepared for burial. The museum places the work within a wider study of Viking Age hoarding. [1] [2]

What remains open

A hoard is not a transparent label for its owner’s identity. The makers, users and people who buried its objects may have been different; the reason for burial requires an argument. [2]
